Clarifying Questions Needed Before PRD

  1. Who is the first paid ICP: solo creators, agencies, B2B SaaS marketers, course sellers, or consultants?
  2. Are we allowed to use third-party YouTube/transcript data providers, or must the platform be official-API-first?
  3. Should credits apply to every AI action, or only expensive actions such as deep reports and thumbnail images?
  4. Is the thumbnail module a lightweight template editor or a serious Canva-like editor?
  5. What is the intended plan mix and pricing: $29/$79/$199, higher, or usage-first?
  6. How long should transcripts, snapshots, generated assets, and reports be retained?
  7. Should users be able to bring their own LLM keys, or should all model usage be managed and resold through credits?
  8. Is 15,000 paying users a 12-month goal, 24-month goal, or architectural ceiling?
